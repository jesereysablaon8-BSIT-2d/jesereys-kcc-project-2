01 ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A legacy rooted in faith

Education that shapes
the whole person.

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. has been part of the educational community of Kabankalan City since 1927.

Guided by its Catholic identity, KCC seeks to nurture learners through academic formation, faith, meaningful relationships, and service to the community. Its commitment is not only to prepare students for a career, but also to help them become responsible and compassionate members of society.
